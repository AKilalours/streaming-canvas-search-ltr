## BACKUP ANSWERS — If They Ask

### "Walk me through the architecture"
> "6 stages. Offline: PySpark 33.8M ratings → 1.29M co-watch pairs,
> 9 quality gates. Stage 1: BM25 + FAISS hybrid retrieval k=2000.
> Stage 2: LightGBM LambdaRank 15 features, 500 trees. Stage 3:
> Cross-encoder BERT top-20, Thompson Sampling exploration.
> Stage 4: FastAPI 106 endpoints, Redis cache, Kubernetes HPA.
> Stage 5: GPT-4o-mini explanations 44 languages, DALL-E 3 diffusion.
> Stage 6: Kafka → propensity logger → retrain at 10K events."

### "What's your nDCG and how did you measure it?"
> "0.8589 at candidate_k=1000 — that's what the eval endpoint reports
> as the conservative baseline. Production uses candidate_k=2000 which
> gives 0.9300 — measured +0.108 nDCG from doubling the candidate pool,
> confirmed in the ablation study. 80/20 split by query_id, no leakage,
> overfit risk LOW per the integrity checks."

### "Design a RAG for 1M PDFs, latency < 1.5s"
> "Clarify first — query or indexing latency? p99 or average?
> Stage 1: BM25 + fine-tuned e5 → 500 candidates, ~200ms.
> Stage 2: Cross-encoder → top-20, ~300ms.
> Stage 3: GPT-4o-mini top-5 chunks → ~700ms.
> Cache on query_hash + chunk_ids in Redis → p50=2.67ms on repeats.
> Total ~1.2s. Latency hides in tokenization 50ms, FAISS 100ms,
> reranker 300ms, LLM 700ms."

### "Explain your diffusion model work"
> "DDPM linear beta schedule — β from 0.0001 to 0.02 over T=1000 steps.
> Forward process adds Gaussian noise. Reverse process: UNet predicts
> noise at each timestep, subtract it, repeat T→0.
> DALL-E 3 uses same DDPM principle in 64×64 latent space —
> VAE encodes to latent, diffusion runs, VAE decodes to 1024×1792 HD.
> Genre-aware prompts: Crime → neo-noir, Sci-Fi → cyberpunk.
> No text in prompt — DALL-E 3 can't render text reliably."

### "Why did your A/B test not ship?"
> "p=0.065 — just above 0.05 threshold. MDE analysis shows I need 3x
> the sample size for 80% power at the observed effect size. That needs
> real users. The offline IPW is correctly implemented — it's underpowered,
> not wrong. I documented it honestly in the README rather than overclaiming."

### "Make it resilient to data drift"
> "9 quality gates before promotion — nDCG drift >5% blocks the pipeline.
> Shadow mode: new model runs parallel 24h without serving, must beat prod
> by 2% before A/B. Rollback: Metaflow artifact versioning, 30 seconds.
> Found 24.6% nDCG gap in pre-2010 content — sparse metadata, quantified
> and roadmapped for LLM-based enrichment."

### "Why LambdaRank over neural LTR?"
> "LambdaRank directly optimises nDCG — not a proxy loss.
> LightGBM inference is microseconds vs 20-50ms for neural.
> Tabular features benefit from boosting over deep models on this corpus.
> I measured both — LambdaRank won."

### "What would you build next?"
> "ALS collaborative filtering as a 4th retrieval signal from the 1.29M
> co-watch pairs. Real-time FAISS updates via Flink — currently needs
> a full batch rebuild. Hard negative mining for fine-tuning —
> estimated +0.03 dense nDCG. Online A/B with real users."
